Rules of the World Line

  1. The Flag Format

    All flags look like recCTF{...} unless a challenge says otherwise. Submit them exactly as found.

  2. Touch Only the Targets

    Attack the challenges, never the platform. The scoreboard, servers and other players are outside the experiment.

  3. No D-Mails Between Teams

    Sharing flags, hints or solutions with other teams is forbidden. What happens in your lab stays in your lab.

  4. No Brute-Forcing the Gate

    Don't spam flag submissions or run heavy automated scanners against the infrastructure unless a challenge asks for it.

  5. One Lab, One Account

    Each team plays from a single account. Multiple accounts to gain an advantage leads to disqualification.

  6. Keep the Timeline Intact

    Write-ups and solutions may only be published after the event has ended.

  7. The Organization Decides

    Organizers' rulings are final. If something is broken, report it — don't exploit it.
